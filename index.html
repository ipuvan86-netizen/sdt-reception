Principle — record how the custom report works
For a later step: letting the app generate the report itself instead of you exporting it. Watching only — nothing is clicked or downloaded for you, and all numbers are saved as #.
How to do it
  1. Press Start recording — Principle opens with a Recording menu at the top of that window. Everything below is done from there, so you never have to come back to this window.
  2. Go to your custom report, the one you normally export.
  3. Open the Predefined Dates dropdown and use Recording → Mark this screen (or Ctrl+M) while the options are showing — that tells Claude whether "next two weeks" already exists.
  4. Set the dates and press Run Report, exactly as you would normally.
  5. Wait for the CSV to finish downloading, then mark the screen again (Ctrl+M).
  6. Recording → Stop and save, then send Claude the file.
PRODA — record how a balance check works
You log in and do the check yourself. This only watches, so it can learn the screens. All numbers are recorded as # and nothing is submitted or automated.
How to do it
  1. Press Open PRODA and log in as normal.
  2. When you're logged in and looking at the main screen, press Start recording. A Recording menu appears at the top of the PRODA window — use that from here on.
  3. Click through to the CDBS balance section and do one check on a practice patient.
  4. Use Recording → Mark this screen (or Ctrl+M) whenever something important is showing — especially the result.
  5. Recording → Stop and save, then send Claude the file.
